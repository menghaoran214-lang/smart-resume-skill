# Growth Metrics · 数据增长型 · 數據成長型

## Identity
A centered name, generous margins and highlighted existing numbers emphasize
evidence for growth operations, ecommerce and commercialization roles.
Channels: Chinese job platforms and agency referrals. ATS: use caution and
verify extraction on the target platform; color is not evidence of compatibility.

## Invariants
A4, 20 mm margins, centered 24 pt name, left-aligned 13 pt section headings.
Body remains 10.5 pt with 16 pt leading. Violet accent on white.
Fine rules introduce sections; all paragraphs retain their full approved text.
Use one full-width reading column; do not make a magazine with tiny text.

## Variation
Validated accent, regular/airy spacing and optional candidate-supplied photo.
Do not copy the fictional demo's section order into unrelated applications.

## Engine and acceptance
`scripts/resume.py`, layout `growth`; `assets/resume.html`, style `growth`.
Inspect rule spacing, centered name clearance with a photo, long body text
and narrow HTML screens. Reject overflow rather than shrinking.

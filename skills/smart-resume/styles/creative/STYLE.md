# Creative Portfolio · 创意作品 · 創意作品

## Identity
A slim violet rail and numbered sections support a recognizable portfolio brief.
Suitable for design, brand and visual roles. Portfolio links must be supported
candidate facts; this layout does not create a portfolio or invent projects.

## Invariants
A4, 6 mm decorative rail outside an 18 mm content margin. 24 pt name, 13 pt
numbered headings, 10.5 pt body with 16 pt leading. One reading column.
No colored background behind body text; no proficiency meters or charts.
Section numbers restart on each page and do not imply achievement rankings.

## Variation
Validated accent and regular/airy spacing. Preserve candidate-provided photo
preferences and all selected evidence. One or two pages.

## Engine and acceptance
`scripts/resume.py`, layout `rail`; `assets/resume.html`, style `creative`.
Inspect rail clearance, heading numbers, long links and every selected claim.

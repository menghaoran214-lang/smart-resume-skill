# Business Executive · 商务精英型 · 商務菁英型

Roles: business development, product operations and project management.
Channels: Chinese platforms, agency referrals and company ATS. Single-column
text is intended for parsing; verify the actual destination. Photo optional.

## Identity
A deep navy top band anchors a quiet full-width executive brief.
Suitable for business and project roles; include leadership only when supported.
Style selection never upgrades a historical title or invents leadership.

## Invariants
A4, 10 mm top band, content starts 18 mm from the page top. 18 mm side margins.
24 pt name, 13 pt headings, 10.5 pt body, 16 pt leading; no text in the band.
Single-column content with a fine header rule. No fake KPI badges.

## Variation
Validated dark accent and regular/airy spacing. One or two pages according to
evidence volume. Keep scope and attribution explicit in approved content.

## Engine and acceptance
`scripts/resume.py`, layout `band`; `assets/resume.html`, style `executive`.
Inspect band clearance, photos, title and continuity on page two.

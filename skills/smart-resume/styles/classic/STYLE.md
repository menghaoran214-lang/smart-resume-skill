# Classic Formal · 经典正式型 · 經典正式型

Roles: finance, traditional firms, management and general professional functions.
Channels: Chinese platforms, company ATS and agency referrals. Conventional
single-column reading order; actual ATS acceptance still needs verification.

## Identity
Left-aligned name and full-width sections separated from the header by a strong rule.
Use for finance, administration and conservative business applications.

## Invariants
A4; 18 mm margins; 24 pt name; 13 pt headings; 10.5 pt body with 16 pt leading.
White paper, dark body text, restrained blue headings. One reading column.
No charts, ratings, decorative icons or invented metrics. A photo is optional.

## Variation
Accent and regular/airy spacing may be saved in a private profile. Keep contrast
at least 4.5:1 on white. Never shrink text to fit.

## Engine and acceptance
`scripts/resume.py`, layout `rule`; `assets/resume.html`, style `classic`.
Inspect header rule, section gaps, glyph coverage and every page bottom.
Demo content is synthetic and separate in `examples/demo.json`.
